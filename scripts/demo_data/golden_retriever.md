# Sunny Afternoon

The big yellow puppy bounded across the open lawn, ears flapping
in the warm breeze. She skidded to a halt by the willow tree,
panting happily, tongue lolling out to one side. Wildflowers
swayed around her paws. Somewhere a child laughed and threw a
tennis ball; she launched after it without a second thought,
fur catching the late summer light like spun honey.

For her, the world was very simple that day: grass, sky, ball,
joy. She did not yet know the word for any of it.
