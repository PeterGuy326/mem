# Travel Diary — Lijiang, July 2012

The old town smelled of rain on warm stone. We climbed the
crooked stairs of Lion Hill before sunrise and watched the
tile rooftops emerge from a sea of mist. Xiao Ming insisted on
trying every street snack we passed: rose cakes, fried goat
cheese, baba bread still hot from the iron griddle.

By the lake at Black Dragon Pool, the Jade Dragon Snow Mountain
floated upside down in the still water. Neither of us said much.
It was the kind of quiet you only get from a place that has been
quiet for a very long time.
