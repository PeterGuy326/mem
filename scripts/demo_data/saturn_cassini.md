# The Grand Finale

After thirteen years orbiting the ringed planet, the spacecraft
threaded itself between the cloud tops and the innermost rim of
particles. No probe had ever flown there. The plasma instrument
tasted the upper atmosphere directly; the magnetometer felt the
gas giant's interior throb.

On the final pass the antenna stayed pointed at Earth until the
last possible second, streaming back data while the small body
tumbled and burned. The mission ended cleanly, a deliberate end
to prevent any biological contamination of the icy moons.
