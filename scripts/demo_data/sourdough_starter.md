# Keeping a Wild Yeast Culture Alive

A jar of bubbling flour-and-water paste is, in chemistry terms,
a balanced ecosystem of lactic acid bacteria and several wild
yeast species cohabiting in equilibrium. Feed it equal weights
of fresh flour and water once a day at room temperature, and
it doubles in volume within four to six hours.

Skip a feeding and the population crashes; refrigerate it and
the metabolism slows to a crawl, buying you a week of neglect.
A spoonful of this living slurry can leaven two kilograms of
dough — no commercial yeast packet required.
